# The Malabar Oils — Minimal Brand Experience

A minimal, professional, and elegant digital presence for **The Malabar Oils** and **The Malabar Farm Foods**, in strategic business association with **Barakkath Marketing, Periyakulam, Theni District, Tamil Nadu**.

---

## 🌿 Core Features

1. **Minimal Luxury Brand Presentation:**
   - Editorial typography (`Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`)
   - Centered around the authentic 3-bottle photography (`public/hero-bottles.jpg`)
   - 3 Signature Guarantee Emblems: **No Adulteration**, **No Preservatives**, **No Chemicals**

2. **Direct WhatsApp Ordering:**
   - Zero unnecessary cart friction or complicated checkout forms.
   - Every "Order on WhatsApp" button pre-fills the exact oil selection, sizes, and pricing directly to Barakkath Marketing.

3. **Dedicated Trade & Bulk Enquiries:**
   - B2B portal for Retailers, Supermarkets, Organic Stores, and HoReCa (Hotels/Restaurants).
   - Instant WhatsApp Trade Lead Generator and interactive query form.
   - Bulk packaging options (5L tins, 15L cans, contract packaging).

4. **Authentic Heritage & Purity Section:**
   - Focuses on traditional Chekku Cold-Pressed (< 38°C cold extraction)
   - AGMARK Grade-1 government certified purity
   - Packed and marketed at Periyakulam, Theni District, Tamil Nadu
